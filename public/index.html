OUR STORY

Born in Amman with an Obsession for Real Smash

Snap Burger started in Amman with one unapologetic philosophy: real food, zero frozen shortcuts. We reject processed frozen patties and artificial tenderizers. Every morning, our butchery team grinds 100% Certified Black Angus beef in-house.

Our potato brioche buns are custom-baked every single morning by local Amman artisan bakeries using real butter.
